# GSAP

## Role
Animation for websites. Timelines, transforms, and sequenced motion. This is the professional motion tool, not a homemade CSS animation.

## Installed
- Source: `upstream/gsap`
- Package: `gsap` 3.15.1 in `tools/website`
- Site use: add `gsap` to the website being built. Do not animate with ad-hoc transitions when GSAP can sequence the move.

## Limits
GSAP does not choose the art direction. Taste does that. Lenis owns the smooth scroll. Do not load every GSAP plugin onto a page that needs one timeline.
