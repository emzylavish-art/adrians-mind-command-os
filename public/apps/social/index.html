PC checks, in order

  1. Key. A wrong key stops before this list. Nothing is written.
  2. Pending draft. An unknown draft stops. Nothing is written.
  3. Money language. Writes only a rejection row. No outbound row. No delivery.
  4. Permanent opt-out. Stops. Nothing is written.
  5. Suppression window. Stops. Nothing is written.
  6. Account state. Killed, paused, or stopped stops. Nothing is written.

If all six pass, the PC writes a queue row and waits for the random gap. A failed confirmation writes a failure row and no outbound row. A confirmed line writes the sent row, then the score update.

Write means a local database row. Send means the saved session confirmed a line.

Wrong key

The PC rejects the call before any read. Write: no. Send: no.

Failed read

One of status, config, opt-outs, or audit fails. The page does not mark Live. Write: no. Send: no.

Closed tunnel

The phone never reaches the PC. The PC does not see the click. Write: no. Send: no.

Clean Approve, not confirmed

All six checks pass, then the session cannot show the line. Write: a failure row. Send: no.

Clean Approve, confirmed

All six checks pass and the saved session shows the line. Write: yes. Send: yes, that line only.

Permanent stop

A clear stop locks the contact. An ordinary toggle cannot clear it. Write: yes. Send: no.
